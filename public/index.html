Period history

Your check-ins

Menstruation

The first day of bleeding marks day one of your cycle.

Follicular phase

This begins on day one and continues until ovulation. It includes your period, even though the circle shows bleeding days separately.

Ovulation

An ovary releases an egg. The app’s calendar can only estimate its timing; it cannot confirm ovulation.

Luteal phase

The time after ovulation and before the next period. Timing and experiences can vary.

Reliable information to help you understand your cycle and talk about changes with a clinician.
